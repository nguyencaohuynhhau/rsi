# ch06: Respect with an Envelope / Give Them a Little Message / Nothing More Than a Mockery (pp. 99–125)

## Question this chapter answers

How do “envelopes of respect,” political–mob messages (Cuba, Bay of Pigs, union wars), and the Get Hoffa legal machine show a system in which justice, loyalty, and public order are routinely mocked?

## Leading propositions

- Orders are never “go hit him”; they are “straighten a matter out,” with local crews staging the work (p. 99).
- Bobby Kennedy’s attorney-generalship is cast as betrayal of the mob–old-man-Kennedy bargain that helped elect JFK (pp. 99–100).
- Seasonal envelopes and verbal notes between Hoffa and Giancana structure respect, debt, and Cuba cooperation (pp. 101–105).
- Sheeran claims he trucked National Guard arms (via Dave Ferrie) to E. Howard Hunt / anti-Castro Cubans before the Bay of Pigs (pp. 104–105).
- “Messages” in the Local 777 rebel war (Abata, Seafarers, lake-dumped cabs) make departure from the Teamsters costly (pp. 108–111).
- Hoffa’s flip-of-a-coin choice of Fitzsimmons over Bobby Holmes is framed as the fatal succession error (p. 107).
- Hoffa allegedly asked Partin about plastic explosives and silencers for RFK; the leak is suppressed at Bobby’s request (pp. 116–117).
- The Test Fleet trial’s real drama is jury tampering observed by a mole while the government also saturates the mob with IRS and wiretap power (pp. 117–124).
- Sheeran is brought to Nashville not for “protesters” but to stare down jurors—“make my presence felt” (p. 124).

## Arguments

- **Premises**: Envelopes at holidays; Russell stops taking Sheeran’s money and gives jewelry; verbal messages carried between Hoffa and Giancana. **Conclusion**: “Respect” is a cash-and-favor economy that doubles as political infrastructure (Cuba, elections, legal help via Ragano) (pp. 101–104).
- **Premises**: Ferrie loads uniforms/weapons from Maryland National Guard; paperwork for the road; delivery to “Hunt” at Orange Grove dog track; later ID as Watergate’s E. Howard Hunt. **Conclusion** (Sheeran/Brandt collage): Hoffa–Bufalino network ran logistics for anti-Castro ops out of “respect” and casino recovery interest—Bay of Pigs failure then re-frames Kennedys as bunglers who stiffed their partners (pp. 104–106).
- **Premises**: Rebels get cards for election; Glimco cannot let Local 777 leave cheaply; Hall charters the winners into AFL-CIO. **Conclusion**: Jurisdictional union war is continuous with organized-crime message violence—same toolkit, different letterhead (pp. 108–111).
- **Premises**: Test Fleet is stale, misdemeanor-level, lawyer-advised; Hoffa still tampers. **Conclusion** (Sheeran): ego wants a clean record; Brandt adds that RFK’s multi-front war raises stakes so a misdemeanor becomes the gateway felony (pp. 117–119).
- **Premises**: Partin passes polygraph; multiple bribe attempts documented; sealed affidavit on Black juror; Sheeran as courtroom intimidation. **Conclusion**: Both sides mock “justice”—Hoffa by buying jurors, the government by planting a rat inside the legal brain trust and building a showdown trial (pp. 116–125).

## Evidence offered

**Brandt framing / public-record material**

- Creation of Get Hoffa Squad under Sheridan (p. 99).
- Church Committee 1975: CIA admits mob help on Bay of Pigs and Castro plots; Giancana killed before testimony; Roselli testifies then is found in an oil drum; *Time* (June 9, 1975) names Bufalino and Giancana (pp. 105–106).
- Partin’s report of Hoffa’s RFK murder talk; polygraph; JFK leak to Ben Bradlee; Bobby kills the story to protect witnesses (pp. 116–117).
- Test Fleet trial structure: Judge Miller; counsel Osborn, Bill Bufalino, Frank Ragano; Chuckie O’Brien and Allen Dorfman in the entourage (pp. 117–119).
- RFK’s post-inauguration multi-agency pooling; IRS “saturation-type investigation”; Roselli and Marcello tax/deportation pressure (pp. 119–120).
- Documented jury approaches: $10,000 to insurance-broker neighbor; fake “reporter Allen”; trooper’s-wife channel; Black juror / Larry Campbell $10,000 story via Partin affidavit (pp. 120–124).
- Photo-insert block mid-chapter (pp. 122–123): visual dossier (Sheeran, Hoffa–RFK, Cheasty sting, commutation papers, Bufalino, Tony Pro, Sally Bugs, Red Fox)—Brandt’s evidence theater interrupting narrative.

**Sheeran oral testimony (voice)**

- Triple-hop day: Puerto Rico (two matters), Chicago (one), San Francisco report; Hoffa yells for not being *early* (p. 99).
- Darby Irish pride in JFK vs. silence around Hoffa; Sinatra cowed by Russell at the 500 Club (pp. 100–101).
- Drive-sleep with Russell; Kelly Mannarino gravy contests; Meyer Lansky intro; morphine favor for Sheeran’s brother (pp. 102–103).
- Dave Ferrie at Gold Coast; arms run Baltimore → Florida; Hunt’s ears later surgically set back (pp. 103–105).
- Sergeant-at-arms 1961 Miami convention; photographer “surgeon” joke; Fitz vs. Holmes coin flip; Tony Pro second VP slot (pp. 106–107).
- Abata message: fifty men, two Philly muscle flee, Sheeran’s “kill me or I’ll kill you” stand; lake-sunk rebel cabs and paper-bag cop payoffs (pp. 109–110).
- Dante’s Inferno: Phalen confesses Lopinson murder-for-hire; Sheeran’s landlady spaghetti alibi (pp. 111–112).
- Marriage to Irene; end of lottery/running (pp. 112–113).
- Crash truck through Seafarers hall plate glass; twenty-six arrests in twenty-four hours; white mice at Horn and Hardart (pp. 113–114).
- Hoffa walks the 107 aisle with “only the Irishman”; wins close election; Test Fleet indictment already hanging (pp. 115–116).
- Nashville: fried chicken, Partin with Ragano unnoticed as “rat,” pellet-gun “nut,” Hoffa charges the gun (pp. 124–125).

**Evidence quality notes (history genre)**

- Church Committee and published IRS/Justice policy are strongest external anchors.
- Ferrie–Hunt arms truck and Sheeran’s presence at Giancana–Hoffa election talk are high-claim, single-source oral history—historically suggestive, not independently proven in the book.
- Partin is a classic compromised witness (own indictments); Brandt leans on Sheridan’s polygraph and later convictions’ legal history without fully airing Partin credibility wars.
- “Mockery” cuts both ways: chapter title targets the trial as farce, but Sheeran’s own message jobs and juror-staring are the street version of the same contempt for formal process.

## Open questions

- Can the Ferrie–Maryland Guard–Hunt trucking story be corroborated outside Sheeran, or is it confessional embroidery on real CIA–mob anti-Castro contact?
- How much of the Abata/Seafarers war violence is Sheeran personal action versus collective folklore of Local 777’s 1961 fight?
- Was Hoffa’s Partin conversation a serious murder solicitation or venting that a desperate government over-read—and does the polygraph settle that?
- Why does Hoffa, with elite counsel and a fair judge, still tamper—pure ego (Sheeran), cultural habit, or rational fear that RFK would never accept an acquittal?
- When Sheeran “makes eye contact with the jurors,” is that actionable intimidation or after-the-fact self-myth as Hoffa’s indispensable Irishman?
